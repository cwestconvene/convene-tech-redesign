Four capabilities. Match one to what you're solving for.

Not every organization needs all four. Here's what each one actually solves.

01Salesforce

Fewer donors lapse when follow-up doesn't fall through the cracks.

  • Grant lifecycle tracking from application through funder reporting, so nothing slips between spreadsheets
  • Donor journeys that flag lapsing supporters before they're gone, not after
  • Board-ready dashboards that don't require a Salesforce login to understand
02ServiceNow

Case management that keeps up with the people you serve.

  • Client and case workflows sized to your program staff, not an enterprise IT department
  • Internal requests and approvals that stop dying in email threads
  • Funder and outcome reporting pulled automatically, not assembled by hand every quarter
03vCISO

Security leadership, without a security department.

  • A fractional CISO fluent in donor data sensitivity, beneficiary privacy, and grant compliance requirements
  • Risk assessments and policies a five-person staff can actually maintain
  • Incident response planning, so one bad week doesn't become the story that defines you to funders
04Custom Development

Tools built for the program you actually run.

  • Purpose-built applications when off-the-shelf software doesn't match how you deliver services
  • Integrations that connect what you already have instead of forcing a costly replacement
  • Built to survive staff turnover, not dependent on the one person who built it
