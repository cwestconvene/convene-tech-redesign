How the partnership works: embedded, not outsourced.

The program runs on two things: full technical ownership, and a cadence that keeps both teams honest.

01
Discovery.A real conversation about your business and where the technical gaps actually are, before any scope is written.
02
Embed.A blended team of US, nearshore, and offshore talent forms around your roadmap, sized to your budget.
03
Cadence.Weekly or biweekly calls keep roadmap, resourcing, and priorities aligned. You stay close to UX and product decisions.
04
Grow together.No hand-off after launch: ongoing maintenance, new features, and the next roadmap, for as long as it works for both sides.
