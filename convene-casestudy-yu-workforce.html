02 The challenge

Three programs. Disconnected systems. One organization needing solutions.

YU Workforce Development runs three distinct programs, CareerPathway, CareerConnect, and Career Labs, each serving students at different stages. Before Convene, these programs operated in silos: student data lived in spreadsheets, onboarding was manual, and staff spent hours on work that should have been automated.

ISS-01Data

Fragmented data

Student records were scattered across spreadsheets, email, and disconnected tools, with no single source of truth across the three programs.

ISS-02Process

Manual processes

Staff onboarding, program assignments, and document collection all required extensive manual intervention, hour after hour.

ISS-03Visibility

No visibility

Leadership had no real-time view of student progress, program outcomes, or cross-program trends to guide decisions.

ISS-04Silos

Three programs, in isolation

CareerPathway, CareerConnect, and Career Labs each ran on their own, so a student's journey never lived in one connected place.

03 The solution

A unified Salesforce ecosystem, built from the ground up.

Convene designed and built a complete Salesforce solution spanning Education Cloud, custom Lightning Web Components, Apex logic, and automated Flow processes, all integrated with YU's existing tools. Two delivery teams worked in parallel across phased sprints, ensuring continuous delivery and zero disruption to ongoing programs.

01 · The student journey

From application to career launch, on one platform.

  • Student self-service portals. Application, enrollment, document upload, and event registration for all three programs, without staff involvement.
  • Every stage connected. Apply, enroll, learn, connect, and launch all live in one system, so no step falls through a gap between tools.
  • Built on Education Cloud. Custom Lightning Web Components and Apex shape each stage around how YU's students actually move forward.
student journey → one connected path
02 · Deep integrations

Connected to your entire technology stack.

  • Six systems, one platform. Box, Jitterbit, FormAssembly, Brightspace, Slack, and Salesforce-to-Salesforce all connect into a single ecosystem.
  • Real-time data sync. Information flows automatically between Salesforce and every connected external tool, no manual re-entry.
  • Staff tools that scale. A custom onboarding center, bulk conversion tools, and program-assignment automation replace hours of manual work.
