vCISO Services

Executive security leadership, without the six-figure hire.

A fractional CISO who sits in your leadership meetings, translates cyber risk into business terms, and owns the compliance program — anchored by a full team of practitioners who do the work behind the strategy.

Security leadership shouldn't wait for a full-time hire.

A dedicated CISO is a $350K+ annual commitment before benefits, tooling, and the team beneath them. A vCISO gives you the same seat at the table — strategic oversight, board-ready reporting, compliance ownership — sized to what your business actually needs today.

How the engagement works

Executive presence. Compliance discipline. Operational depth.

A Convene vCISO brings all three — not a solo advisor who consults on strategy but stops at the whiteboard, and not a compliance auditor who ships a report and moves on.

Executive presence

Your vCISO joins leadership meetings, translates risk into business terms your board understands, and owns the annual security narrative — without you needing to build cyber fluency from scratch.

Compliance discipline

NIST 800-171, ISO 27001, SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI-DSS — whichever frameworks apply, we build the program to sustain them, not just pass an audit and go back to reactive mode.

Operational depth

Behind the CISO sits a full team of practitioners — identity engineers, cloud security architects, incident responders. Strategy that ships, not strategy that stalls.

The real problem

Companies rarely arrive at "we need a CISO" on their own timing.

Usually a customer contract, an insurance renewal, a regulator, or an incident forces the conversation — and by then the calendar is unforgiving. A vCISO fills the gap without the twelve-month executive search.

Customer security questionnaires

Every enterprise deal now comes with a 200-question security review. Without a CISO to own it, the answers come out inconsistent, sales stalls, and deals slip a quarter.

Cyber insurance renewal

Carriers now require documented controls, MFA everywhere, incident-response plans, and evidence they're tested. Miss a control and premiums double — or coverage disappears.

Regulatory pressure

New rules keep arriving — SEC cyber-disclosure, state privacy laws, sector-specific frameworks. Without a CISO tracking the landscape, exposure quietly compounds.

Board asking hard questions

"How are we protected? What's our risk? Are we insured?" — and no one at the executive table has the credible answer. A vCISO becomes that answer.

Post-incident cleanup

Something happened. The board wants a program that ensures it doesn't happen again. A vCISO leads the remediation, builds the operating model, and stays through the next audit cycle.

M&A due diligence

Buying or being bought? Cyber due diligence is now table-stakes. Missing controls or unmanaged risk lower valuations. A vCISO surfaces and closes the gaps ahead of the review.
