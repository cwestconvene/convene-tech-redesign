The commercial Salesforce challenge

What we actually hear.

"Too many leads, inconsistent qualification, SDR churn, stale cadences, unclear attribution, and we need to improve conversion and sales-cycle efficiency."

Lead volume

More leads than the team can qualify well, so good prospects slip through while reps chase the wrong ones.

Inconsistent qualification

Different SDRs work from different definitions of "qualified," so the pipeline means different things to different people.

SDR churn

Turnover breaks pipeline continuity: ramp time resets, relationships reset, and momentum is lost with every seat that turns over.

Stale cadences

Outreach scripts that haven't matured with the buyer, so response rates quietly decline while no one notices why.

Unclear attribution

It's hard to tell which channel actually closed the deal, so budget decisions get made on hunches instead of evidence.

Cycle efficiency

Deals take longer than they should, and without the right signals, no one can say exactly where they're stalling.
