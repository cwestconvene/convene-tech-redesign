02 The challenge

A clear vision, no technical path to build it.

intice knew the problem it wanted to solve: moving auto shoppers off their computers and into dealership showrooms. Turning that into working software was another matter.

ISS-01Capability

No in-house technical expertise

intice's founders knew marketing and the auto industry cold, but lacked the engineering resources to execute their product vision.

ISS-02Risk

Build vs. buy uncertainty

Hiring an internal team was slower and riskier than partnering with a company with proven experience shipping applications.

ISS-03Budget

Cost-efficiency at scale

Any technical partner needed to stay within budget without slowing down the pace of product innovation.

ISS-04Trust

Long-term reliability

This wasn't a single project. intice needed a partner willing to think and commit like a co-founder, not a vendor.

03 The solution

A technical co-founder, not a vendor.

Convene joined intice as a technical co-founder from day one, leading application development and staying embedded in strategy ever since.

01 · Embedded co-founder model

Product decisions, made together.

  • Regular strategy calls. Ongoing meetings to assess and reassess strategy, roadmap, and resourcing keep both teams aligned.
  • Shared UX & interface input. intice's team contributes directly to user-experience and interface decisions. This is a creative partnership, not a hand-off.
  • Long-term ownership. Convene cares about intice's long-term success, providing guidance on architecture, maintenance, and support as the product grows.
co-founder loop → shared roadmap
02 · Flexible global delivery

Cost-efficient, without slowing down.

  • Blended talent model. A mix of nearshore, offshore, and U.S.-based talent keeps delivery within budget while still driving innovation.
  • Speed to product. intice consistently praises Convene's ability to get to a working product fast, with reliable, timely deliverables.
  • Full-stack ownership. From patented lead-conversion tools to showroom-incentive applications, Convene leads development end to end.
talent blend → delivery
